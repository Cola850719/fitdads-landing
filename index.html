Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FitDads

Do I need any equipment to get started? +

No! Many of our most popular workouts use just your bodyweight. We do offer modifications for those with dumbbells or other equipment, but it's completely optional.

What if I have joint problems or injuries? +

Our coaches provide modifications for all exercises. You can also specify any injuries or limitations during onboarding, and the AI will personalize everything accordingly.

How much time per week do I need to commit? +

Our programs are flexible. Most members see results with just 3-4 workouts per week of 12-30 minutes each, plus simple nutrition guidelines. You're in control of your schedule.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +

Absolutely. No contracts, no complicated cancellation process. You can cancel your subscription anytime from your account settings. We just ask for feedback to help us improve.

Is there a money-back guarantee? +

Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee with our Pro and Elite plans. If you're not satisfied, we'll refund your money, no questions asked.

How personalized are the workouts? +

Very! Our AI learns from your performance data, preferences, schedule, and feedback to continuously optimize your workouts for maximum results and sustainability.
